# Historical Cost-Schedule Qualification

The delivery-cost schedule is incomplete and unusable for the pilot.

| Component | Classification | Status |
|---|---|---|
| STT | statutory | UNKNOWN for complete 2024 interval |
| Exchange transaction charge | exchange tariff | UNKNOWN |
| SEBI charge | regulatory | UNKNOWN |
| Stamp duty | statutory | Source category identified; schedule unqualified |
| Service tax/GST | statutory tax on services | UNKNOWN |
| Brokerage | assumption | Separately versioned, not statutory |
| Spread/slippage/impact | execution assumption | Separately versioned, not statutory |

SEBI circular MRD/DoP/SE/Cir-14/2009 establishes that exchange transaction charges have dated revisions, but does not complete every component or interval. Unknown intervals fail; current rates are never carried backward. Future rows require effective dates, rate, base, buy/sell applicability, official citation, schedule version, and authoritative supersession where known.
