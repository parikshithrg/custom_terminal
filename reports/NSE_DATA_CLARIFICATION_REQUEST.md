# Request for written clarification on public NSE historical files

To: `marketdata@nse.co.in`  
Research routing copy: `nseri@nse.co.in`

Subject: Clarification request — manual access and non-commercial retention of dated NSE cash-market files

Dear NSE Data / Economic Policy & Research team,

I am building a personal, non-commercial market-research system for Indian equities. It does not execute trades, redistribute raw NSE data, or provide a commercial data service. Its purpose is to test whether historically reproducible research can be performed without survivorship or look-ahead bias.

The specific evidence required is the official dated NSE cash-market bhavcopy together with the dated cash-market MII security file for twelve fixed sample dates between 2016 and 2025. I understand from NSE circular NSE/MSD/60315 that website dissemination of `NSE_CM_security_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz` began on 5 February 2024, and I understand that NSE's current Terms of Use prohibit systematic automated collection. I have stopped automated acquisition and am requesting clarification before doing anything further.

Could you please confirm in writing:

1. Whether an individual may manually download dated public bhavcopy and MII security files for personal, non-commercial research.
2. Whether exact files may be retained locally and immutably with hashes and retrieval metadata for reproducibility.
3. Whether normalized local tables, aggregate diagnostics, and unpublished derived research may be created and retained.
4. Whether corrected or revised files may be retained alongside prior vintages.
5. Whether official dated cash-market security snapshots are available for 4 Jan 2016, 24 Aug 2016, 6 Feb 2018, 21 Sep 2018, 2 Jan 2020, 23 Mar 2020, 24 Feb 2022, 17 Jun 2022, and 2 Jan 2024 through any non-paid public or research route.
6. If available, the approved manual access path and file nomenclature.
7. Whether access requires payment, subscription, institutional affiliation, an NDA, declaration, research proposal, or other agreement.
8. The restrictions that apply to displaying aggregate results and to redistribution of raw or normalized records. Raw files will not be published without express permission.

The project cannot use paid market-data providers. If the requested evidence is available only through a paid product, a confirmation of that fact would also be very helpful.

Thank you for directing this request to the appropriate team if another NSE department owns these permissions or archives.

Sincerely,

Project owner, `custom_terminal`
