# One-Year Pilot Readiness

Calendar year **2024** is selected for the readiness specification: it is recent and complete, A.6 proved one official 2024 bhavcopy format, and it was not selected from a research outcome.

The pilot was **not executed**. It is blocked because dated historical security snapshots, archive-retention permission, qualified NIFTY 50 PRI and TRI downloads, complete 2024 costs, and a lifecycle-coverage method are not established.

It requires 100% of declared sessions for price and security files, PRI and TRI, costs, and zero unresolved price/security keys. It aborts on CAPTCHA/access control, authentication, rate limiting, unclear retention terms, a missing mandatory session, an unknown schema transition, or content mutation. Acquisition is content-addressed and cached, at least one second apart with two retries, and never bypasses controls.

Estimated acquisition is about 500 daily objects for roughly 250 paired sessions plus bounded event objects. This is planning only. See `specs/one_year_archive_pilot_2024_v1.json`.
